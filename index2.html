Semiconductors

The foundation of all modern technology

×
Semiconductor Opportunity

India has the potential to capture 10% of the global semiconductor market by 2030.

  • Current global market: $550 billion
  • Projected growth: 8% annually
  • India's current share: <1%

Government initiatives like the India Semiconductor Mission are providing $10 billion in incentives.

Artificial Intelligence

Reimagining how we interact with technology

×
AI Innovation Opportunity

India is uniquely positioned to develop AI solutions for diverse global markets.

  • Global AI market: $150 billion
  • Expected to reach $1.5 trillion by 2030
  • India's AI talent pool: 2nd largest globally

Opportunities in healthcare, agriculture, education, and manufacturing sectors.

Space Technology

The final frontier of innovation

×
Space Tech Frontier

India's space program has proven it can achieve more with less.

  • Global space economy: $450 billion
  • India's Mars mission cost: $74 million (10x cheaper than NASA)
  • Private space startups in India: Growing rapidly

Opportunities in satellite technology, launch services, and space applications.

Electric Vehicles

Revolutionizing transportation

×
EV Revolution in India

India is rapidly adopting electric mobility.

  • Global EV market: ~$384 billion (2023)
  • Expected CAGR: Over 20% in the next decade
  • India's EV target: 30% of all vehicles by 2030
  • Government incentives: FAME India scheme

Opportunities in battery technology, charging infrastructure, and indigenous EV design & manufacturing.

Robotics

Automating for efficiency and precision

×
Robotics & Automation Potential

Robotics is poised to transform various Indian sectors.

  • Global robotics market: ~$85 billion (2023)
  • Expected to reach ~$200 billion by 2030
  • India's industrial robot density: Low but growing
  • Applications in manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture, and logistics

Opportunities in industrial automation, medical robotics, service robots, and AI-powered robotics.

Quantum Computing

The next computing revolution

×
Quantum Frontier

Quantum computing represents a fundamental shift in computing capability.

  • Global quantum computing market: ~$0.6 billion (2023)
  • Projected to reach ~$65 billion by 2030
  • India's National Quantum Mission: ₹6,000 crore (revised budget)

Opportunities in quantum algorithms, quantum-secure communications, and sensing technologies.
